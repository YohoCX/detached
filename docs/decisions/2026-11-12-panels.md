# Multi-agent panels inspired by revmux, the full scope

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-12.

- Detached runs groups of agents over a subject: review, pre-merge gate, triage, plan review,
  dialectic and brainstorm. Ideas come from revmux; the design is our own.
- The full scope ships, with no v1 cuts: every mode, mixed models on any role, statistics by lens
  and model from the owner's own dispositions, and Fix in a managed worktree.
- A good progress UI is part of the scope.
- Why: the owner uses these reviews daily and wants them inside the client, done right once.
- Rejected:
  - copying revmux;
  - a viewer of revmux's archive only;
  - the reviewer's advice to cut dialectic, brainstorm, mixed models, per-lens stats and Fix from
    the first version.
- The engine is a stage graph, so the modes are configurations of one engine.
