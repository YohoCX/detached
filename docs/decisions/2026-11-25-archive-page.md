# Archive is a page, the zones go

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-25, from screenshots of 0.7.1 on main.

- The Archive button shows one page in the live session's center card: archived sessions, past chats and archived
  tabs with their category filters. Sessions and Explorer stay as in a live session.
- The legacy dock goes: no Left, Right or Bottom zone, no zone toggles or New chat button in the title bar, no
  archive-only title bar. Every app those zones held opens as a tab in the center.
- Why: one button opened three panels and a page; the side panel and the title bar icons looked like stray UI
  ("убрать это говно просто, убрать всё лишнее, подчистить").
- Rejected: keeping the archive mode with its zones closed by default; it keeps a second shape of the app.
